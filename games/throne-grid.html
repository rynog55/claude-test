Tap a square and name a character who fits both the row on the left and the fact on top. Rows and columns can be great houses, groups like the Night's Watch or the Kingsguard, or a character's fate. You get 9 guesses, and each character can be used once.

Only the HBO series counts (seasons 1 to 8), not the books or the spin-offs. A house counts by birth, or by marrying in and taking the name, so Catelyn is a Tully and a Stark; Jon Snow counts as Stark and Targaryen; Joffrey, Tommen and Myrcella count as Baratheon and Lannister. "Dies on screen" includes Jon Snow, who died in season 5 and came back. "King or queen" includes rulers and claimants. Easy uses the bigger houses, the Night's Watch, the Kingsguard and the Small Council; Hard adds Martell, Tully, Bolton and the Free Folk. The list covers about 110 characters compiled by hand, so a character who fits might be missing. This is a fan-made game and isn't affiliated with HBO. Easy sticks to the bigger bands and well-known facts, and every square has at least 2 right answers. Hard adds every band, more band types, and Spotify's mood, key, energy and popularity ratings. Your guesses are saved on this device.
