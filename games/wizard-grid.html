Tap a square and name a character who fits both the row on the left and the fact on top. Rows and columns can be houses, groups like the Order of the Phoenix or Dumbledore's Army, or traits like Animagus or Quidditch Seeker. You get 9 guesses, and each character can be used once.

Easy uses the four houses, the Order, Dumbledore's Army, the Weasleys and Hogwarts staff. Hard adds Death Eaters and the Ministry, plus more traits. Facts come from the seven books, not the films or later stories, and a fact only counts when the books state it, so a character whose house or blood status is never given doesn't count for those squares. "Dies in the books" means during the story, so James and Lily Potter don't count. The list covers about 130 characters compiled by hand, so a character who fits might be missing. This is a fan-made game and isn't affiliated with J.K. Rowling or Warner Bros. Easy sticks to the bigger bands and well-known facts, and every square has at least 2 right answers. Hard adds every band, more band types, and Spotify's mood, key, energy and popularity ratings. Your guesses are saved on this device.
