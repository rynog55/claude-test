Tap a square and name a player who fits both the row on the left and the fact on top. Rows are Kentucky head coaches; a player counts for every coach he played for. Columns are titles, NBA draft results, honors and more. You get 9 guesses, and each player can be used once.

Players are everyone on a Kentucky roster from 1930–31 (Adolph Rupp's first season) to 2025–26, from wildcatworld.com's season rosters. Draft results and NBA All-Star selections come from Wikipedia's NBA draft pages, and honored jerseys, all-time top 10s, Hall of Famers and Olympians from Wikipedia's Kentucky page. "One-and-done" means one season on the roster, then drafted that year. "Top 10 all-time" means a top-10 spot in points, rebounds, assists, steals or blocks. Easy uses Rupp, Hall, Pitino, Tubby Smith and Calipari; Hard adds Sutton, Gillispie and Pope plus deeper columns. This is a fan-made game and isn't affiliated with the University of Kentucky. Easy sticks to the bigger bands and well-known facts, and every square has at least 2 right answers. Hard adds every band, more band types, and Spotify's mood, key, energy and popularity ratings. Your guesses are saved on this device.
