Tap a square and pick a player who played college ball at the school on the left and fits the category on top. You get 9 guesses, and each player can be used once. National champion, Final Four MOP and National POY count only at the school where they happened. Draft and NBA categories count for any school the player attended.

The player list has every NBA draft pick from 2000 to 2025 (from Wikipedia's draft pages), plus notable earlier stars and title-team players. For drafted players, the school is the last one they attended before the draft, so earlier stops on a transfer may be missing. Your guesses are saved on this device.
