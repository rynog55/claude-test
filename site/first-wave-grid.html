Tap a square and name a song from your new wave playlist that fits the band on the left and the fact on top. You get 9 guesses, and each song can be used once.

The answer list is the songs on your Spotify playlist of classic new wave, synth-pop and post-punk heard on satellite radio. This game isn't affiliated with SiriusXM. Length, popularity, danceability, mood, key and genre come from Spotify's data. Popularity was measured when you exported the playlist: "Deep cut" is the least-played quarter and "Spotify smash" the most-played quarter. US top 10 and UK No. 1 hits come from Wikipedia's chart pages. Years use the song's original release, and band facts were tagged by hand. Medium sticks to the bigger bands and well-known facts, and every square has at least 2 right answers. Hard adds every band, more band types, and Spotify's mood, key, energy and popularity ratings. Your guesses are saved on this device.
