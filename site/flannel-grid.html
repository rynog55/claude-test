Tap a square and name a song from your '90s alt-radio playlist that fits the band on the left and the fact on top. You get 9 guesses, and each song can be used once.

The answer list is the 327 songs on your Spotify playlist of tracks heard on SiriusXM's '90s alternative channel. This game isn't affiliated with SiriusXM. Length, popularity, label, explicit flag and the mood, energy, key and acoustic ratings come from Spotify's data. Popularity was measured when you exported the playlist: "Deep cut" is the least-played quarter and "Spotify smash" the most-played quarter. Modern Rock and Mainstream Rock No. 1s come from Wikipedia. Years use the song's original release, and band facts were tagged by hand. Medium sticks to the bigger bands and well-known facts, and every square has at least 2 right answers. Hard adds every band, more band types, and Spotify's mood, key, energy and popularity ratings. Your guesses are saved on this device.
